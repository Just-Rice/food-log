🍽 Food Log

This is not medical advice. It is a food diary built on public nutrition data. It sets no diet targets, and photo estimates are rough until a portion is measured. If you are managing a health condition, are pregnant, or have had a difficult relationship with food, talk to a doctor or dietitian before using any tracker.

Foods, photographs and reference intakes come from Food is Medicine: nutrition figures from USDA FoodData Central, daily targets from the NASEM Dietary Reference Intakes, photographs from Wikimedia Commons, credited where shown. Your log stays in this browser.
